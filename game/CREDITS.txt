Gameplay B7:
- Liên Quân-style: left joystick, ATK + S1/S2/S3 cooldown rings, ULT
- Nội năng (mana) under HP; skills cost mana; empty mana → question refill
- Ultimate requires HARD question (do_kho>=3); Q card animates in/out BEFORE cinematic
- Boss AI: chase + red ground telegraph dodgeable
- Longer road riddles (b7-rid-*) with pinyin + Vietnamese
- localStorage save schema=1 (SAVE_SCHEMA)
- speechSynthesis zh-CN; if no zh voice → show pinyin instead of audio
- Perf: pixelRatio<=1.5, default MID mobile, shadow map cut, particle/dmg pools,
  renderer.compile warm, bloom/FXAA off on LOW, fewer karst draws
